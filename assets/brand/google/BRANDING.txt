Google "G" logo
===============

Source:  https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png
         (downloaded 2026-08-18, original 200x204 RGBA)

Bundled as 1x/2x/3x density buckets, each the mark fitted inside a square
canvas with transparent padding, which is how Google's own button spec places
it. The mark itself is unmodified apart from scaling.

This is a Google trademark, not open-licensed content. It is bundled solely to
label the "Continue with Google" button, which is the use Google's Sign in with
Google branding guidelines require:

  https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines

Under those guidelines the mark must not be recoloured, redrawn, rotated, or
otherwise altered, and must not be used to suggest Google endorses Kith. Do not
reuse it anywhere else in the app.
